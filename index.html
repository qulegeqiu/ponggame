PONG Controller

A clear acrylic pentagon tube that plays Pong when you shake it.

Swipe to learn how to play →

Which side is which?

Hold it upright. The end on top picks the direction.

🌙 Moon on top = UP
⭐ Star on top = DOWN

Tilt chooses the direction, shaking is what moves the paddle!

Shake it any way — up and down or side to side.
🌙 on top → the paddle goes up. ⭐ on top → the paddle goes down.

Example

1. The ball is flying to the top.
2. 🌙 is on top, so shake → your paddle climbs up and hits it!
3. Next ball goes lower? Flip it so ⭐ is on top, then shake → the paddle goes down.

Your turn — try it!

Pick 🌙 or ⭐, then shake: tap Shake, or wiggle the controller with your finger. Catch the ball!

📱

Play with your real phone?

Your phone becomes the controller: hold it upright for 🌙, turn it upside down for ⭐, and shake to move the paddle.

You've got it!

Have fun!

Grab your controller and play a round of Pong.
